# audioarxiv

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Turn arXiv papers into audio. audioarxiv lets you fetch the research papers from arXiv and read them aloud.

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/isaac-cf-wong/audioarxiv
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/audioarxiv/
