# apkpull

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Automate downloading (pulling) Android apps from Google Play, across multiple real/emulated devices at once.

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/david-lev/apkpull
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/apkpull/

- 1.0.0

- 下载页面：https://pypi.org/project/apkpull/#files
- 运行要求：Python >=3.10
