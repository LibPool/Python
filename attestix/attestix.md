# attestix

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Attestix - Attestation Infrastructure for AI Agents. DID-based agent identity, W3C Verifiable Credentials, EU AI Act compliance, delegation chains, and reputation scoring. 47 MCP tools across 9 modules.
