# aviconv-asterix

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Bidirectional EUROCONTROL ASTERIX codec: CAT010 (surface movement), CAT011 (A-SMGCS data), CAT020 (multilateration), CAT021 (ADS-B), CAT034 (monoradar service messages), CAT048 (monoradar target reports), and CAT062 (system tracks) to/from JSON.
