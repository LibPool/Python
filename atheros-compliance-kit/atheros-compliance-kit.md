# atheros-compliance-kit

> 标签: Python

## 简介

AtherosAI Compliance Kit — EU AI Act evidence and ISO/IEC 42001 records for five named clauses from inside your own codebase and CI: RAG bias and data-quality scoring, third-party LLM guardrails, risk classification with Annex IV dossiers, and third-party vendor assessment. English and Turkish.
