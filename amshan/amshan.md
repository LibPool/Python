# amshan

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Decode P1 and MBUS (Meter Bus) DLMS data. Special support for norwegian and swedish meters.

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/toreamun/amshan
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/amshan/
