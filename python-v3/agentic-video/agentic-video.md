# agentic-video

> 标签: agents, contact-sheet, ffmpeg, mcp, video, whisper

## 简介

Local video tools for AI agents that read text and images but not video: contact sheets, transcripts, cut, loudness and sync checks, served over MCP.

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/tlq5l/agentic-video
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/agentic-video/
