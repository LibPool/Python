# orbit-crm

> 标签: ai-agents, claude, contact-management, crm, deduplication, email, entity-resolution, knowledge-base, llm, local-first, markdown, mcp, mcp-server, model-context-protocol, obsidian, obsidian-md, personal-crm, pkm, sqlite

## 简介

Personal CRM MCP server: AI agents keep your network as an Obsidian vault of plain Markdown notes. People, organizations, emails and meetings, tasks and topics, with duplicate detection, email threads and a source for every fact. Local-first: no cloud, no LLM inside.
