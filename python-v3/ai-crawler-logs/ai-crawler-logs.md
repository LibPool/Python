# ai-crawler-logs

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Access-log triage for AI crawlers: identify every line, attribute it to its operator, and generate a robots.txt or WAF rule for the traffic you actually received. 150 crawlers bundled, six log formats auto-detected, zero dependencies.
