# pamir

> 标签: Python

## 简介

PaMIR: Public Arrival-ordered Measurement for Inference in Risk — an open benchmark for credit-default prediction with scarce and delayed labels: 19 public datasets, label-delayed streaming and i.i.d. evaluation, leakage-controlled synthetic augmentation
