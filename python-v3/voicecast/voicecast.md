# voicecast

> 标签: Python

## 简介

VoiceCast Studio from your terminal or AI agent: narrated videos (16:9 or 9:16 Shorts) in an AI voice or your own cloned voice. Sign in, write, build, review, publish.

## 官网

- 官网：https://voicecast.studio
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/voicecast/
