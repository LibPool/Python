# navig-video

> 标签: Python

## 简介

NAVIG Video module — render a channel's video formats (kinetic episodes, bulletins, motion cards) from its workspace of texts, plates and voice: timeline → frames → ffmpeg, with per-destination encode variants. A first-party navig plugin (free, toggleable).
