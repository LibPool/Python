# mechanics-ai999x

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Offline classical-mechanics solver: damped, driven and coupled oscillations, pendulums, orbits, moments of inertia, rolling, Atwood machines, precession, rotating frames, collisions and rockets, with steps, LaTeX and honest refusals
