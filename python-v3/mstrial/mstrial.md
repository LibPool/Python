# mstrial

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Multiple sclerosis trial design and endpoints: ARR, time to confirmed worsening, NEDA, negative binomial sample size for lesion counts, MRI schedules under a budget, PIRA power through the whole pipeline, and the endpoint landscape of registered trials.
