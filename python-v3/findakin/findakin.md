# findakin

> 标签: anomaly-detection, astronomy, clustering, embeddings, lancedb, mcp, similarity-search, spectra, spectroscopy

## 简介

Population-level questions on collections of astronomical spectra: similarity search, clustering, anomalies, trajectories and encoder benchmarks on a local LanceDB store, with embeddings from spectro-kernel.
