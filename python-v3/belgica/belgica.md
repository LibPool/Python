# belgica

> 标签: arrondissements, belgian, belgium, courts, geojson, interest-rates, judicial-cantons, justice-of-the-peace, legal, municipalities, postal-codes, refnis, vredegerecht

## 简介

Belgian legal reference data: courts, judicial cantons and arrondissements, municipalities and postal codes, and legal and commercial interest rates.
