# mocai

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Mocai from your terminal and your AI agent: turn video files into motion capture (FBX, GLB, USD, BVH) with one command, or run a local MCP server.

## 官网

- 官网：https://mocai.ai
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/mocai/
