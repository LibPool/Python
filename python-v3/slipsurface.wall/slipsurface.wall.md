# slipsurface.wall

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Cantilever reinforced concrete retaining walls: earth pressure by Rankine, Coulomb, at rest and the trial wedge; sliding, overturning, eccentricity, bearing capacity by Terzaghi, Meyerhof, Hansen, Vesić and EN 1997-1; global stability by Bishop and Fellenius; the earthquake of TBDY 2018; the reinforced concrete design of TS 500 with the bars, the reinforcement drawing (DXF) and the bar bending schedule, with PDF reporting.
