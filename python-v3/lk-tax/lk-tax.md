# lk-tax

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Sri Lankan tax rules as code: APIT, income tax, WHT, VAT and SSCL, effective-dated and sourced to the Inland Revenue Department.

- 官网：https://github.com/ShalomHunukumbura/lk-tax
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/lk-tax/
