# viora-ai

> 标签: Python

## 简介

viora: AI assistants that talk, in English and Swahili. A website/app assistant that answers from your own information, a Jarvis-style voice assistant for your computer that thinks with Claude Code, opencode, Codex, Gemini or any model, background tasks, and an MCP server that gives any AI a voice. By INCPRITECH.
