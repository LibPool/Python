# jupyter-jitters

> 标签: data-science, fun, ipython, ipython-extension, jupyter, jupyterlab, monkey, notebook, tamagotchi

## 简介

Jitters the VibeCoder, an anxious monkey, under your notebook cells: sweating when a cell raises, groggy when one runs past a minute, terrible at the first cell after midnight. %load_ext jitters; sends nothing.
