# scigantic-facebase

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Search FaceBase, the craniofacial research data hub, and read its open-access micro-CT, imaging and sequencing data from Python: a typed catalog over FaceBase's open metadata API, citations in the form its Terms of Use ask for, ranged reads of remote files, and single-slice reads from zipped micro-CT stacks without downloading them.
