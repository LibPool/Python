# mediapane

> 标签: ghostty, images, kitty, matplotlib, ssh, terminal, tmux, video

## 简介

A media sidecar for your terminal: keep one pane open and push images, GIFs and videos to it from anywhere.

- 官网：https://github.com/hematteo/mediapane
