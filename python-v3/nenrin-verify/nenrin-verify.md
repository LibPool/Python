# nenrin-verify

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Recompute NENRIN evidence yourself, offline: A2A task provenance, two-party agreement records, TSUGI recovery chains and MUSUBI contracts, and whether a door's signed admissions in a mirror form one history (nenrin-track). Record every A2A call your agent makes as a signed witness record (two lines, official A2A SDK), and apply your own rule to a resume.
