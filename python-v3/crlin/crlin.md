# crlin

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Verified linear algebra over NumPy arrays: enclosures of exact solutions (dense, banded and sparse systems, interval data, least squares, very ill-conditioned systems) and of symmetric eigenvalues, and proofs of positive definiteness; safe with a threaded BLAS; on crnumerics' libcrlin
