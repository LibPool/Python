# audiobook-reader

> 标签: Python

## 简介

AI-powered audiobook generator with GPU/NPU acceleration (up to 10x faster than real-time). Built-in Kokoro-82M TTS with character-aware voices and dialogue detection. Interactive chapter stripping to remove unwanted content. Supports EPUB, PDF, TXT, MD, RST (use convertext for DOCX/MOBI/HTML).
