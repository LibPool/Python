# tiktok-live-agency

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Unofficial TikTok LIVE agency SDK by tik.tools: find creators to recruit, track diamonds and live status across your roster, and build your own agency dashboard. Not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or ByteDance.
