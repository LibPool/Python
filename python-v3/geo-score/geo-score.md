# geo-score

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Open-source AI visibility (GEO) scorer for websites, by Jianrun Tech; formerly geo-watch. Can AI engines cite your site, and do they? A free 0-100 readiness score against an open rubric, plus citation checks through the AI engines' APIs with your own keys.
