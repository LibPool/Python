# md-to-word-docx

> 标签: csl, docx, docx-generator, latex, markdown, ooxml, report, thesis, typography, word

## 简介

Deterministic Markdown to Word (.docx) compiler: declared typography, native equations, tables, cross-references, CSL bibliographies, pre- and post-export quality gates.
