# brillq-opex-benchmark

> 标签: Python

## 简介

brillQ OPEX Benchmark: Bayesian cost-frontier benchmarking of operating costs with credible intervals, an evidence gate, explainable drivers and client reports (HTML, Excel) in English and Polish. Commercial software by brillQ.
