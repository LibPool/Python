# roadster-ai-video

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Official client SDK and prompt metadata helper for Roadster AI Video (https://roadster-ai-video.com) - Turn single car photos into Tesla Roadster-style stunt videos.

## 官网

- 官网：https://roadster-ai-video.com
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/ryanellis42675/roadster-ai-video
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/roadster-ai-video/
