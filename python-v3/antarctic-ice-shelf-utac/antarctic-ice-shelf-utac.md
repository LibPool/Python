# antarctic-ice-shelf-utac

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Real Antarctic ice shelf mass budget (Davison et al. 2023), Holocene meltwater-feedback mechanism (Nature Geoscience 2025), CMIP6 regional East/West asymmetry (Muilwijk et al. 2026), plus six more real mechanisms (subglacial hydrology, Southern Annular Mode, sea-ice albedo feedback, seismic-ice interactions, black carbon deposition, ENSO teleconnection), no UTAC/CREP/AFET bridge
