# dock-timelapse

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Record your macOS Dock over time and render Apple-style timelapse videos of its evolution (CLI + MCP server).

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/bcldvd/dock-timelapse
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/dock-timelapse/

- 下载页面：https://pypi.org/project/dock-timelapse/#files
