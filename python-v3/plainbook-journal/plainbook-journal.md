# plainbook-journal

> 标签: Python

## 简介

A free trading journal that lives on your machine, for forex, futures, stocks, crypto and prop firm traders: plain markdown files, R-multiple statistics, MFE and MAE, prop firm rules, playbooks and reports. No cloud, no account, no dependencies. Windows, macOS, Linux.
