# pyxpo-pose-detection

> 标签: Python

## 简介

On-device human pose detection for pyxpo apps: 17 body landmarks from a photo or every frame of a video (ML Kit on Android, Vision on iOS).

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/pyxpo/pyxpo/tree/main/packages/pose-detection
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/pyxpo-pose-detection/
