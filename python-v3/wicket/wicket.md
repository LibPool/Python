# wicket

> 标签: admission-control, asyncio, concurrency-limit, fair-queuing, fairness, multi-tenant, postgresql, priority, rate-limiting, scheduling, work-queue

## 简介

Fair admission of work to shared capacity: which waiting ticket may start now, by priority, fairness between tenants, and limits on concurrency and rate.
