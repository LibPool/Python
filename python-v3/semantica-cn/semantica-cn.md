# semantica-cn

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Community Chinese fork (semantica-cn) of Semantica — Graph-Native Infrastructure for Context and Accountable AI Systems: context graphs, decision intelligence, full provenance tracking, and explainable reasoning engines — every AI decision traceable, every output auditable.
