# lifedb

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Local-first personal timeline DB — locations, photos, workouts, sleep on one time axis. Your data never leaves your machine.

- 官网：https://github.com/SPA3K/lifedb
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/lifedb/
