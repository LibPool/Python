# filefit

> 标签: compress, converter, docx, jpg, pdf, resize

## 简介

Convert JPG, PNG, PDF and DOCX files and shrink them under a size limit for upload portals.

## 官网

- 官网：https://dipanjanbanerjee.github.io/filefit/
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/dipanjanbanerjee/filefit
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/filefit/
