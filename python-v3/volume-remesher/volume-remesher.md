# volume-remesher

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Exact volume meshing of triangle soups: polyhedral and tetrahedral meshes, booleans, surface embedding and 2D segment arrangements, with provenance tracking

- 官网：https://github.com/wildmeshing/VolumeRemesher
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/volume-remesher/
