# eprdating

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Open ESR dating of tooth enamel in Python: EPR and HPGe gamma spectra, dose rates with one-group beta attenuation, EU/LU/US-ESR/CSUS-ESR ages with Monte Carlo uncertainties; validated against ROSY, DATA and published ages.
