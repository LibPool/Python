# ew-energy-models

> 标签: ashrae-guideline-14, emis, energy, global-adjustment, ici, ieso, measurement-and-verification, ontario

## 简介

Open, auditable energy-analytics models from Enerwyse: load analytics, M&V baselines, and Ontario Global Adjustment / ICI peak tools. Every result ships with an evidence receipt.
