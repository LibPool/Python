# cloakwall

> 标签: audit-log, compliance, gdpr, guardrail, hipaa, litellm, llm, phi, pii, redaction

## 简介

PII/PHI redaction and tamper-evident audit logging for LiteLLM. In-process, no sidecar, works air-gapped.
