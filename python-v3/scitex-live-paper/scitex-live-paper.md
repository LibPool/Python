# scitex-live-paper

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Interactive, AI-verifiable live rendering of research manuscripts (consumer of scitex-clew claim model). Part of SciTeX.

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/ywatanabe1989/scitex-live-paper
