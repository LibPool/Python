# voicecast-worker

> 标签: Python

## 简介

VoiceCast Worker: lend your Mac to VoiceCast Studio and make your own videos without limits. Speaks narration (sonic-forge) and renders videos for the studio's job queue while you are away, draws your own pictures with your Gemini key or FLUX.2 klein, saves your finished videos to ~/Movies/VoiceCast; the parrot twins in your menu bar show what it is doing.
