# kronos-quantum

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Kronos Quantum — honest, runnable quantum-computing-for-fusion codes: nine quantum programs that run on a simulator today and on real hardware the moment you plug in an account. No quantum advantage claimed this decade; a meaningful role in fusion is a post-~2036 prospect.
