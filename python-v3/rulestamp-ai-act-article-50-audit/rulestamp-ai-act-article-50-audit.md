# rulestamp-ai-act-article-50-audit

> 标签: ai-act, article-50, chatbot, compliance, transparency

## 简介

Finds the AI call sites, generation code and system prompts that trigger EU AI Act Article 50 disclosure duties - in force since 2 August 2026. (bundled Node.js runtime)
