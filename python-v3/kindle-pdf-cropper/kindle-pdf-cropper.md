# kindle-pdf-cropper

> 标签: crop, ebook, ebook-reader, epub, footer, header, kindle, margins, pdf

## 简介

Auto-crop PDF margins / headers / footers and optionally split pages into Kindle-friendly landscape pages (PDF or EPUB output)

- 官网：https://github.com/ygnid/kindle-pdf-cropper
