# india-market-holidays

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Indian market and bank holidays and business day adjustment (NSE, BSE, MCX, RBI city bank calendars) via the marketholidays.in API.

## 官网

- 官网：https://marketholidays.in/api-docs
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/BOZXYZ/india-market-holidays
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/india-market-holidays/
