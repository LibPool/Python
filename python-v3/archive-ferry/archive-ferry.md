# archive-ferry

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Archive your YouTube channel to the Internet Archive (archive.org): resumable, one video at a time, with a local web UI.

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/Abusadat18/archive-ferry
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/archive-ferry/

- 下载页面：https://pypi.org/project/archive-ferry/#files
- 运行要求：Python >=3.10
