# polyweave-gamekit

> 标签: 3d, assets, blender, claude-code, gamedev, godot, mcp, sound

## 简介

Make a game's parts by declaring what each must satisfy: 3D assets, pictures, sound loops and the world's text, on top of Blender, Godot and paid generators.
