# lektor-ai-chatbot

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Add the Asyntai AI chat assistant to your Lektor site. It answers visitor questions from your own content.

## 官网

- 官网：https://asyntai.com/documentation/integrations/lektor/
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/asyntai/lektor-ai-chatbot
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/lektor-ai-chatbot/
