# PyPDF2

> 标签: PDF, 解析

## 简介

A pure-python PDF library capable of splitting, merging, cropping, and transforming PDF files

## 官网

- 官网：https://pypi.org/project/PyPDF2/
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/py-pdf/PyPDF2
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/PyPDF2/
