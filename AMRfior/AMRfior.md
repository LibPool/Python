# AMRfior

> 标签: Python

## 简介

AMRfior: A toolkit that uses BLAST, BWA, Bowtie2, DIAMOND, and Minimap2 to search DNA and protein sequences against AMR databases (DNA and AA) such as CARD/RGI and ResFinder.
