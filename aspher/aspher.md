# aspher

> 标签: Python

## 简介

ASPHER — Accelerated SPectral and HiERarchical contact solver: BEM normal contact on an elastic half-space (exact Love/Boussinesq kernel, H-matrix and matrix-free H2/FMM operators, FFTW |q| spectral preconditioner, nested-grid mixed-precision Polonsky–Keer CG)
