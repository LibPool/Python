# ag2-tealtiger

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Deterministic governance adapter for AG2 (AutoGen fork) — policy enforcement, PII detection, cost tracking, per-agent kill switch, governed speaker selection, and structured audit evidence. No LLM in the governance path.
