# agents-u-cash

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Monetize AI agents with non-custodial HTTP-402 crypto payments. Zero-dependency Python client for agents.u.cash: sell priced resources, get paid to your own wallet across 40+ chains, with scoped subagent keys.
