# agentmesh-ai

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Open-source, self-hosted observability for AI agents: OTLP ingestion, tracing SDK, sessions, cost analytics, datasets and LLM-as-judge experiments, alerts, replay, and an MCP server. SQLite or PostgreSQL.
