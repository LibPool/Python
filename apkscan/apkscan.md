# apkscan

> 标签: Python

## 简介

Scan for secrets, endpoints, and other sensitive data after decompiling and deobfuscating Android files. (.apk, .xapk, .dex, .jar, .class, .smali, .zip, .aar, .arsc, .aab, .jadx.kts)
