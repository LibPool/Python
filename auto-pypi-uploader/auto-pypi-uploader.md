# auto-pypi-uploader

> 标签: Python

## 简介

A program to automate the creation of the 'setup.py' file, changing a pip package's version, & publishing it to PyPi.

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/Huckdirks/auto-pypi-exporter
- PyPI 项目页：https://pypi.org/project/auto-pypi-uploader/
