# atoa-agent-pay

> 标签: Python

## 简介

The ergonomic, standalone Python SDK for agentic payments: embed payments in your product (or give your AI agent a payment tool) under a signed, capped, payee-scoped contract over HTTP. Three nouns — agent, contract, payment.
